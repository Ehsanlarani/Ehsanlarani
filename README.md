1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

During the first operational season, the Financial Team will focus on building a secure, transparent, and scalable financial foundation for the organization. Rather than prioritizing expansion, the team's primary objective is to establish governance, financial discipline, compliance, and reporting systems that support sustainable long-term growth.

The finance department will operate under the leadership of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and consist of three core members, each with clearly defined responsibilities while maintaining cross-functional collaboration.

3. TEAM ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Primary Mission:
Lead the organization's financial strategy, governance, capital planning, and long-term financial sustainability.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop financial strategy aligned with organizational objectives.
- Build financial governance framework.
- Design financial policies and procedures.
- Approve budgets and expenditures.
- Manage organizational cash flow.
- Present financial reports to executive leadership.
- Oversee fundraising and investor financial communication.
- Monitor financial risks.
- Supervise audits.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Lead strategic financial planning.
- Approve internal financial controls.
- Monitor key financial performance indicators (KPIs).
- Coordinate with legal and executive teams.
- Develop long-term financial sustainability plans.

3.2 Financial Operations & Accounting Officer

Primary Mission:
Maintain accurate financial records while ensuring daily accounting operations are properly executed.

Key Responsibilities:
- General bookkeeping
- Journal entries
- Expense recording
- Revenue recording
- Account reconciliation
- Monthly financial closing
- Asset management
- Invoice processing
- Payroll coordination
- Financial documentation
- Maintain accounting software
- Support audit preparation
- Prepare monthly financial statements
- Maintain supporting documentation

3.3 Treasury, Budget & Compliance Officer

Primary Mission:
Protect organizational financial resources through budgeting, treasury management, and regulatory compliance.

Key Responsibilities:
- Prepare departmental budgets
- Budget monitoring
- Cash flow forecasting
- Treasury management
- Bank account management
- Payment authorization workflow
- Expense approval verification
- Financial compliance monitoring
- Internal control implementation
- Tax coordination
- Regulatory reporting
- Vendor payment scheduling
- Financial risk monitoring

6. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

Governance:
- 100% policy completion
- Approval workflows implemented
- Financial authority matrix operational

Accounting:
- 100% transaction recording accuracy
- Monthly close completed within five business days
- Zero unreconciled accounts

Budget:
- Budget variance maintained within ±5%
- Monthly budget reviews completed
- Departmental budget compliance achieved

Treasury:
- Cash flow forecast updated weekly
- No delayed vendor payments
- Minimum operating cash reserve maintained

Reporting:
- Monthly reports issued by the agreed deadline
- Quarterly executive reports completed
- KPI dashboard updated monthly

Compliance:
- Zero regulatory violations
- Successful internal control assessments
- Audit-ready financial documentation

8. RESPONSE TO THE ORIGINAL REQUEST

Financial Team Structure and First-Season Strategic Plan (Three-Member Team)
The Financial Team will operate under the leadership of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and consist of three members responsible for establishing the organization's financial foundation during the first operational season. The team's strategic priorities include implementing financial governance, budgeting, accounting, treasury management, compliance, financial reporting, and internal financial controls.

The CFO will oversee financial strategy, governance, executive reporting, and long-term planning. The Financial Operations & Accounting Officer will manage day-to-day accounting activities, bookkeeping, financial records, reconciliations, and reporting. The Treasury, Budget & Compliance Officer will oversee budgeting, cash flow management, treasury operations, regulatory compliance, and internal financial controls.

The first season will focus on creating standardized financial policies, deploying an accounting system, establishing budgeting and reporting frameworks, implementing internal controls, ensuring regulatory compliance, and building transparent financial processes that support sustainable organizational growth. By the end of the season, the finance department will be fully operational, audit-ready, and equipped with governance structures and performance metrics to support future expansion.
