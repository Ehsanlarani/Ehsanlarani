## 👨‍💼 About Me

I am **Ehsan Larani**, a financial executive focused on building secure, transparent, compliant, and scalable financial systems.

As a **Chief Financial Officer**, I work at the intersection of financial strategy, corporate governance, technology, risk management, and organizational growth.

My work focuses on transforming ambitious ideas into financially structured and sustainable operations through disciplined planning, reliable reporting, responsible treasury management, and strong internal controls.

> **My objective is to create financial foundations that support innovation while protecting organizational resources, maintaining accountability, and enabling sustainable long-term growth.**
